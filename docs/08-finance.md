# Payments, settlement and commercial model
Production money movement is blocked until D04–D08 and D15 approved. Use a licensed/authorized marketplace PSP with seller onboarding, split allocation, refunds and payout support; verify UAE availability and contractual merchant model before selecting. Do not implement platform-owned escrow, wallet or direct transfers. Sandbox adapter only; clearly label transactions test mode. If PSP cannot support multi-seller checkout, restrict live checkout to one seller rather than inventing settlement.

## Calculation contract
Each line: quantity × tier unit price, rounded to fils using approved rounding method (sandbox half-up). Shipping per seller fixed by accepted delivery quote. Tax based on configured tax codes, invoice issuer and approved tax-inclusive/exclusive policy. Sandbox fixture: 100 units × AED 10 = AED 1,000, shipping AED 50, configured fixture tax 5% on both = AED 52.50, total AED 1,102.50. Fixture commission 10% on merchandise excl tax/shipping = AED 100; commission tax and PSP fees modeled separately, not assumed zero in production. With fixture commission tax 0 and fees 0, seller payable AED 1,002.50. This is a test example, not a UAE tax determination or pricing recommendation. Use decimal arithmetic, never JS Number for money; allocate rounding remainder deterministically and verify sum of orders = payment total.

Store price, commission rule version, tax rule version, seller terms, address and invoice issuer snapshots. Compute commission on approved base, never on a client-supplied amount. Refund allocation by order and original line values, reversals linked to original entries. Reject cumulative refunds above capture; reconcile fees and nonrefundable amounts per signed policy.

## Lifecycle
Create checkout and reservation; PSP create session with checkout idempotency key; browser returns pending. Verify signed webhook and exact amount/currency/reference; durable inbox then worker processes with row locks. On successful capture allocate to orders, convert reservations to stock deductions, append ledger and notify. On failure release reservation only when provider confirms no capture or session cancelled; expiry triggers provider query/cancel, not blind release. Late capture after released stock: quarantine and route to refund/reconciliation; never mark fulfilment automatically. Duplicate and out-of-order events are no-ops or reconciled against provider authoritative state. Payments and payout states separate.

Ledger is operational reconciliation, not statutory accounting. Example capture: debit PSP clearing 110250, credit seller payable 100250, credit platform fee revenue 10000 (fixture above). On provider settlement reconcile clearing against payout/fee amounts; finance must approve chart of accounts and timing of revenue recognition. Every journal balances; append reversal rather than edit. Disputes place internal settlement eligibility on hold; actual provider holds only if contract supports them. A provider payout event—not admin click—marks paid out. Refund request requires independent approver and PSP confirmation; do not show refunded until confirmed. Daily reconcile captures, fees, refunds, payouts against provider statement; flag missing/duplicate/mismatched records; audited resolution.

Invoices: distinguish seller commercial/tax invoice, PSP receipt, platform fee invoice and subscription invoice; issuer and numbering policy must be approved by accountant. Store immutable issued document, corrections via approved credit note process. Do not claim tax compliance merely from template. Never collect full card or bank details; hosted provider onboarding/payment methods only.

## Membership and revenue
Free verified access in sandbox MVP. Plan versions define audience, billing interval, price, active listing limit, RFQ allowance and feature entitlements. Paid access, commissions, sponsored placements and sourcing fees are configurable but disabled until approved. Entitlements enforced server-side with atomic usage counters (phase 2); plan downgrade never deletes listings or existing orders. Failed renewal restricts new gated actions only according to approved policy; existing order/support access retained. Subscription events from PSP are authoritative. No hardcoded production rate.
