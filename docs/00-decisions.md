## Phase 3 provisional decisions (pending founder confirmation)
The founder's standing instruction (27 November 2026 UTC) is to continue every phase without per-phase approval. The rules below were needed to build Phase 3 (RFQs and quotes); none was approved by the founder. They are implemented as configuration or isolated rules so they can change without data loss. Status: PENDING FOUNDER.

| ID | Provisional decision | Why this default | Affected docs | Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P3-1 | RFQ audience: the buyer chooses "matching suppliers" (POOL) or "invited suppliers only" (INVITE). POOL reaches sellers whose company is approved, has a public storefront and has at least one public product in the same top-level category; invited sellers can always see the RFQ. A company never sees or quotes on its own RFQ. | Lets buyers reach the market while keeping invite-only sourcing; matches A12 (trading needs approved business) | 02-buyer, 03-seller, 09-workflows | `rfqs.visibility`, `rfq_invites`; `poolEligible` SQL in `apps/api/src/rfq/rfq-common.ts` |
| P3-2 | Limits: deadline 60 minutes to 90 days ahead and no later than the target date; up to 20 invitees, 5 attachments per RFQ and per quote version, quote validity up to 90 days, 50 open RFQs per buyer workspace. No cap on the number of quotes per RFQ (one quote per supplier). | Abuse protection without blocking normal sourcing | 02-buyer, 07-api | `RFQ_LIMITS` in `packages/contracts/src/rfq.ts` |
| P3-3 | Quote tax: supplier chooses STANDARD, ZERO_RATED or EXEMPT; the platform computes tax on subtotal plus shipping with half-up rounding to the fil. STANDARD uses `TAX_STANDARD_BPS` (500 in development; production refuses to start without an explicit value). This is not tax advice; D06 (invoice issuer, VAT treatment) stays open. | D06 forbids assuming a blanket treatment; the rate is configuration, not code | 08-finance, 07-api | `quote_versions.tax_code/tax_bps/tax_fils`; `quoteTotals()`; env check |
| P3-4 | Deadline behaviour: an RFQ stays OPEN after its deadline while at least one submitted quote is still valid, so the buyer can still award; the worker expires lapsed quotes and then RFQs with no valid quote. After the deadline a supplier may revise only when the buyer asked for a revision. | Buyers often decide after the deadline; avoids losing valid offers | 09-workflows | `runRfqExpiry` (every minute) |
| P3-5 | Award: the buyer accepts one exact quote version (Idempotency-Key required); other open quotes are declined; the payment deadline starts at acceptance (A05, `QUOTE_PAYMENT_WINDOW_HOURS`). The order and checkout are created in Phase 4, so `order_id` stays empty and nothing is charged. | A05; Phase 4 owns orders and payment | 09-workflows, 08-finance | `rfqs.accepted_quote_version_id` (unique), `idempotency_keys` |
| P3-6 | Messaging: buyer and seller workspaces message each other only when one side is an approved buyer and the other a public seller; text only in Phase 3 (attachments later); new-message emails are off by default, RFQ and quote emails on. | A12 (commercial communication needs approved business); avoid email noise | 02-buyer, 03-seller, 10-security | `conversations`, `messages`, `read_receipts`, `notification_preferences` |
| P3-7 | Admin oversight: support and compliance staff see RFQ metadata and quote states, never quote prices or documents; compliance can hide or restore an RFQ with an audited reason. | Commercial confidentiality between buyer and supplier | 04-admin, 10-security | Capabilities `admin.rfqs.read`, `admin.rfqs.moderate` |
