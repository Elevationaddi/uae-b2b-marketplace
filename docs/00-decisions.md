## Phase 5 provisional decisions (pending founder confirmation)
Built under the founder's standing instruction to continue without per-phase approval. None of these was approved by the founder. Refunds and payouts go only through the labelled sandbox provider adapter; no live PSP, payout rail or bank transfer is connected and nothing is marked paid by hand (D05 stays open). Status: PENDING FOUNDER.

| ID | Provisional decision | Why this default | Affected docs | Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P5-1 | Fulfilment: one shipment per order covering all ordered quantities (no partial shipments). The supplier records carrier, optional tracking reference and expected date, then reports delivery with an optional proof file (PDF/PNG/JPEG, malware-scanned, private). Only paid orders can be processed or dispatched (T24). Shipping actions need `seller.inventory.ship` (OWNER, WAREHOUSE) by role, even while the company is restricted, so paid orders can still be delivered. | Simplest model that matches seller-arranged delivery (D07); restriction must not strand buyers' paid goods | 03-seller, 09-workflows | `shipments`, `shipment_items`; `FulfilmentService` |
| P5-2 | Completion: an order completes only when the buyer confirms receipt (OWNER or PROCUREMENT). There is no automatic completion after N days. A completed, undisputed order becomes eligible for payout; nothing is paid automatically (T25). | Avoids paying suppliers for goods the buyer never confirmed; auto-completion is a founder choice | 02-buyer, 08-finance | `orders.completed_at`, `orders.settlement_state` |
| P5-3 | Disputes: either party may open one active dispute per paid order (types: not received, damaged, wrong items, short quantity, quality, other) until 14 days after receipt is confirmed and never after the supplier is paid out. An active dispute holds payout eligibility (T27). Messages and evidence are append-only; up to 200 messages. Support assigns, requests evidence and proposes either no action (support closes) or a refund (finance executes; the dispute closes when the provider confirms). | Gives buyers a clear window and keeps money decisions with finance | 02-buyer, 03-seller, 04-admin, 09-workflows | `disputes`, `dispute_messages`; `DisputeService`; `FULFILMENT_LIMITS` |
| P5-4 | Refunds: requested by FINANCE, approved by a different FINANCE_APPROVER (self-approval refused at API and database level, T28); cumulative requested and confirmed refunds never exceed the order total (T29); refunds are refused once the order is in a payout or paid out. Each refund reverses the platform commission pro-rata (half-up) and the rest from the seller payable. The ledger posts only on the provider's confirmation; duplicates and out-of-order events are ignored (T30); failures open a reconciliation case. A full refund of an unshipped order cancels it. | Dual control for money movement (docs/08-finance.md); commission follows the refunded share | 04-admin, 08-finance | `refunds`; `processSettlementEvent` in `packages/db/src/settlement.ts` |
| P5-5 | Payouts: finance requests a payout of all eligible, undisputed orders for one supplier; a different finance approver approves; the worker sends it to the provider adapter with an idempotency key. Orders are marked paid out only on the provider's paid event; a failure releases the orders back to eligible and opens a reconciliation case with no paid label (T31). No schedule or minimum amount. | D05 (payout model) is open; manual, dual-approved batches are the safest default | 04-admin, 08-finance | `payouts`, `payout_allocations`; `settlement.submit` job |
| P5-6 | Reviews: one rating (1–5) with an optional comment per completed order, by the buyer (T39). Content cannot be edited or deleted (database trigger); COMPLIANCE can hide or republish with an audited reason. Public storefronts show the average and recent reviews as "Verified buyer", never the buyer company name, so procurement relationships are not disclosed. | Protects buyer confidentiality; moderation without rewriting what buyers said | 01-product, 04-admin | `reviews`; `GET /marketplace/suppliers/{id}/reviews` |
| P5-7 | Seller finance view: OWNER and FINANCE see gross paid value, commission, refunds, net payable and amounts not yet eligible, eligible, held, in payout and paid out, plus their payouts. | Suppliers need to reconcile without seeing other companies' data | 03-seller, 08-finance | `GET /seller/workspaces/{ws}/finance` |
